# Sulfide solid electrolyte interface challenges (硫化物固态电解质)
DEMO ONLY — synthetic teaching note, not a paper or a patent; no experimental measurements are claimed.
Domain: solid-state batteries, sulfide electrolyte, lithium metal interface.
Sulfide solid electrolytes are investigated for high ionic conductivity and mechanical processability. However, sulfide materials can be moisture-sensitive and may release hydrogen sulfide when exposed to water, so processing conditions and safety controls matter.
At the lithium metal interface, chemical instability and interphase formation can raise resistance. Protective coatings, interlayers, engineered electrode interfaces and contact-pressure management are typical research directions. High interfacial resistance is a key failure mode that should be measured with impedance spectroscopy.
研究要点：硫化物电解质离子电导率高，但空气湿度敏感、界面副反应、界面阻抗与锂金属接触是主要挑战。对比材料时需要考虑空气稳定性、界面稳定性、制造成本和实际循环数据。
Research validation: Compare electrochemical impedance spectroscopy and cycling under matched pressure, temperature and electrode loading. A single conductivity number is not enough to establish battery performance.

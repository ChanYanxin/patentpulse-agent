Research note (paraphrase): This granted US patent describes a robotic end-effector with a hierarchical sensing architecture. Groups of sensors mounted at gripper fingers observe contacts with objects, including signals associated with force or pressure, thermal changes, vibration and proximity. Local processing circuits organize the information and communicate to a central processing circuit. Tactile feedback can inform gripper actuator control or the broader robot controller. It addresses the engineering challenge of moving multimodal tactile signals from fingers through manageable electronics and processing layers.

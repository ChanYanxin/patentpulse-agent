# Silicon carbide power semiconductor design (碳化硅功率半导体)
DEMO ONLY — synthetic teaching note, not a paper or a patent; no experimental measurements are claimed.
Domain: SiC MOSFET, power electronics, wide-bandgap semiconductor.
Silicon carbide (SiC) is a wide-bandgap semiconductor used in high-voltage power electronic applications. SiC MOSFET research addresses switching efficiency, on-resistance, gate oxide reliability, defects and thermal management. Gate oxide interface traps and threshold-voltage stability are relevant to device qualification.
Manufacturing considerations include substrate defects, epitaxial quality, wafer yield and packaging reliability. Evaluation must distinguish device-level switching loss from converter-level energy efficiency.
研究要点：SiC 碳化硅功率器件需要关注 MOSFET 栅氧可靠性、阈值漂移、导通电阻、开关损耗与封装热管理。

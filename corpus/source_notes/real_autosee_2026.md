# Source note: AutoSEE solid-electrolyte research agent (2026)
TYPE: Independently source-identified PUBLICATION SUMMARY, written for TechIntel demo; NOT the paper full text. Verify the publication directly before quoting results.
SOURCE: Feifei Luo et al., "AutoSEE: An Artificial Intelligence Agent for Automated Solid Electrolyte Exploration," ACS Energy Letters, 2026, 11(4), 3369-3376.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1021/acsenergylett.6c00020
The AutoSEE researchers describe a solid electrolyte discovery workflow controlled by GPT-4o with literature screening, text mining, and machine learning capabilities. The published abstract reports processing 226 papers and mean precision of 93.4% in five minutes for the selected halide-electrolyte case study.
The paper reports a discovered design correlation relating high charge density to amorphous phases and ionic conductivity, followed by experimental evaluation of a candidate composition, LiTaB0.32O0.98Cl5, with reported ionic conductivity of 7.82 mS/cm.

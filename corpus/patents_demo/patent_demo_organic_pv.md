# DEMO-PAT-P01 — organic photovoltaic donor-acceptor film

SOURCE STATUS: SYNTHETIC TRAINING RECORD. NOT AN ACTUAL PATENT. NO PUBLICATION NUMBER.

Technical field: organic solar cells. A donor-acceptor semiconductor blend is deposited on a conductive substrate using a low-temperature coating process with controlled solvent removal.

Claims (synthetic and illustrative only):
1. An organic photovoltaic device comprising a donor-acceptor active layer disposed on a transparent conductive electrode and a charge extraction layer.
2. The organic photovoltaic device of claim 1, wherein a low-temperature annealing step controls active layer morphology.
3. The organic photovoltaic device of claim 2, wherein a barrier coating reduces oxygen ingress.

# Polymer electrolyte temperature and flexibility (聚合物电解质)
DEMO ONLY — synthetic teaching note, not a paper or a patent; no experimental measurements are claimed.
Domain: polymer solid-state electrolyte, PEO, ionic transport.
Polymer electrolytes can provide compliant contact with electrodes and potentially easier manufacturing through coating or film processing. Polyethylene oxide (PEO) is a widely studied host. Segmental mobility and crystallinity affect lithium-ion transport and operating-temperature behavior.
Compared with brittle ceramic electrolytes, flexible polymer films may simplify contact, but room-temperature ionic conductivity, oxidative stability and mechanical dendrite suppression can remain important bottlenecks. Composite polymer electrolytes combine polymer phases with ceramic fillers to explore compromises.
研究要点：PEO 聚合物电解质柔韧、薄膜加工方便，但室温电导率和结晶度影响明显，需要比较温度依赖性、力学性能及界面接触。
Research validation: Measure conductivity over temperature and assess cycling stability with matched cell protocols.

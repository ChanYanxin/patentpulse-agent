# Lithium metal dendrite risk and short-circuit diagnostics (锂枝晶)
DEMO ONLY — synthetic teaching note, not a paper or a patent; no experimental measurements are claimed.
Domain: lithium metal, dendrite, safety, short circuit diagnostics.
Lithium dendrite-like growth and filament penetration are potential failure mechanisms in lithium-metal systems. A solid electrolyte is not automatically immune to penetration: defects, grain boundaries, localized current density and mechanical contact can influence failure.
Diagnostics include post-mortem microscopy, critical-current studies, voltage traces and impedance spectroscopy. A short circuit may reflect several interacting failure mechanisms; a single test does not prove a unique causal pathway.
研究要点：锂枝晶可能造成短路，需结合临界电流密度、显微图像、缺陷位置和电化学阻抗来诊断，而不是仅凭充放电曲线下结论。
Research validation: Vary stack pressure, plating current, electrolyte thickness and material defects, with suitable replication.

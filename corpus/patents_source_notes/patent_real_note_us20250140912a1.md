Research note (paraphrase): The patent application describes an argyrodite-type sulfide electrolyte, modified by elemental doping, to address two connected challenges: moisture instability and ionic transport. The disclosure refers to doping with a tetravalent element and a hexavalent transition-metal component and discusses suppressing formation of hydrogen sulfide from hydrolysis while maintaining or improving ionic conductivity and interfacial behavior in solid-state batteries. This is a different material strategy from an internally oriented crystal region in US20240243351A1.

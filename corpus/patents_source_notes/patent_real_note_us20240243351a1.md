# US20240243351A1 — Sulfide solid electrolyte and all solid state battery

SOURCE TYPE: MANUALLY PARAPHRASED PUBLIC PATENT METADATA / ABSTRACT, NOT THE FULL PATENT.

Publication date: 2024-07-18
Primary source for human verification: https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240243351A1/en

Research note (paraphrase): This US patent application concerns sulfide solid electrolytes for solid-state batteries and the deterioration of lithium-ion conductivity after contact with atmospheric moisture. Its disclosed approach includes a lithium/phosphorus/sulfur/oxygen-containing sulfide electrolyte with a granular morphology and an inward surface crystal region. The proposed structure is intended to mitigate moisture-related losses in ionic conductivity. The disclosure connects the material design to electrode and electrolyte layers of all-solid-state cells.

Keywords: sulfide solid electrolyte all solid state battery moisture humidity lithium ionic conductivity internal crystal portion granular morphology Li P S O resistance water stability.

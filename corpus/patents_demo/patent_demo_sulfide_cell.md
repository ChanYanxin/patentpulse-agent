# DEMO-PAT-S01 — sulfide solid-state cell moisture-barrier enclosure

SOURCE STATUS: SYNTHETIC TRAINING RECORD. NOT AN ACTUAL PATENT. NO PUBLICATION NUMBER.

Technical field: solid-state lithium battery architecture. A layered cell uses a sulfide electrolyte, a moisture barrier membrane, an anode current collector, and a cathode composite.

Claims (synthetic and illustrative only):
1. A solid-state electrochemical cell comprising a sulfide solid electrolyte, a cathode composite and a moisture barrier housing.
2. The electrochemical cell of claim 1, wherein the housing comprises an aluminum laminate and a desiccant indicator.
3. The electrochemical cell of claim 1, wherein an interfacial buffer layer reduces contact resistance between the sulfide electrolyte and the cathode.

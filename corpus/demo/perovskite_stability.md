# Perovskite photovoltaic interface passivation (钙钛矿)
DEMO ONLY — synthetic teaching note, not a paper or a patent; no experimental measurements are claimed.
Domain: perovskite solar cells, defect passivation, environmental stability.
Perovskite photovoltaic devices may show strong light absorption, but environmental stability, ion migration and interface defects are important engineering issues. Surface passivation targets non-radiative recombination associated with defects.
Encapsulation, standardized illumination testing and moisture stability studies are required before claiming long operational lifetimes. Passivation improvements observed in one device architecture may not transfer to another.
研究要点：钙钛矿太阳能电池需要分析界面钝化、非辐射复合、离子迁移、水氧稳定性和封装寿命。
Research validation: Clearly report humidity, illumination, temperature and stabilization protocol.

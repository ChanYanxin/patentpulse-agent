# Source note: LISA solid-state-battery domain-specific RAG assistant (2025)
TYPE: Independently source-identified PUBLICATION SUMMARY, written for TechIntel demo; NOT the paper full text. Verify the publication directly before quoting results.
SOURCE: "LISA: A Lithium-Ion Solid-State Assistant using large language models for knowledge defragmentation in battery science and beyond," Materials Today Communications (2025).
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mtcomm.2025.112380
LISA is presented by its authors as a domain-focused, retrieval-augmented assistant for solid-state battery research. Its retrieval system selects relevant segments from domain literature and supplies them to an LLM to answer researcher queries.
For TechIntel, this is a useful example of RAG for scientific literature, distinct from tool-execution agents that drive external simulations or experiments. Technical evaluation numbers for LISA should be taken from the original paper rather than inferred from this summary.

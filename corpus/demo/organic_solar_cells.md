# Organic solar cell donor-acceptor materials (有机半导体太阳能电池)
DEMO ONLY — synthetic teaching note, not a paper or a patent; no experimental measurements are claimed.
Domain: organic semiconductor, organic photovoltaics, donor acceptor.
Organic photovoltaics rely on organic donor and acceptor semiconductors. Frontier molecular orbital levels, absorption range, morphology and charge-carrier mobility influence device performance. Non-fullerene acceptors are an active research topic in organic solar cells.
Machine learning and quantum chemistry can help screen candidate molecules; however, predicted molecular properties are not substitutes for thin-film morphology studies or measured power conversion efficiency. Data leakage from near-duplicate molecules can inflate benchmark performance.
研究要点：有机半导体太阳能电池关注给体受体材料、HOMO LUMO 能级、光吸收、形貌、载流子迁移率。量子化学加机器学习可用于候选分子筛选。
Research validation: Use scaffold-aware splits, uncertainty estimation and independent experimental validation.
